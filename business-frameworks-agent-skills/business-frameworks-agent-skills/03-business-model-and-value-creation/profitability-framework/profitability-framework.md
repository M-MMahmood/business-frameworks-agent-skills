# Profitability Framework

## Purpose
Use this skill to diagnose why profits changed or how to increase them.

## When to use
- The user names Profitability Framework explicitly.
- The task matches: diagnose why profits changed or how to increase them.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Decompose Profit = Revenue - Costs.
2. Revenue = Price x Volume (split by product, segment, channel).
3. Costs = Fixed + Variable (split by category).
4. Compare to history, budget, and competitors to isolate the driver.
5. Drill down on the main driver, find root causes, and size levers.
6. Recommend actions with impact and feasibility.

## Output format
Profit tree with numbers, root-cause hypothesis, and ranked levers.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Boiling the ocean; failing to compare against a benchmark; not being MECE.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
