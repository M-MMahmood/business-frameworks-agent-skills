# Business Model Canvas

## Purpose
Use this skill to map or design how a business creates, delivers, and captures value.

## When to use
- The user names Business Model Canvas explicitly.
- The task matches: map or design how a business creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Fill the nine blocks: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, Cost Structure.
2. Start with segments and value propositions; check fit.
3. Verify consistency between blocks (e.g., channels fit relationships).
4. Identify assumptions and risks; propose experiments or alternative models.

## Output format
Nine-block canvas in a table plus a top-5 assumptions list.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Filling blocks in isolation; vague segments; no testing of assumptions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
