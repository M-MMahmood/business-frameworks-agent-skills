# SMART Goals and 5W1H

## Purpose
Use this skill to turn intentions into specific, accountable plans.

## When to use
- The user names SMART Goals and 5W1H explicitly.
- The task matches: turn intentions into specific, accountable plans.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Write the goal; test it as Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound.
2. Rewrite until each criterion passes.
3. Detail the plan with 5W1H: What, Why, Who, When, Where, How.
4. Define milestones, dependencies, and risks.

## Output format
Refined SMART goal and a 5W1H action table.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Vague verbs; unrealistic targets; no owner.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
