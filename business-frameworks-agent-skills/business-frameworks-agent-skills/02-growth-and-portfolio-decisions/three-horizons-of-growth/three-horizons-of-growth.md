# Three Horizons of Growth

## Purpose
Use this skill to balance defending the core, building new businesses, and seeding future options.

## When to use
- The user names Three Horizons of Growth explicitly.
- The task matches: balance defending the core, building new businesses, and seeding future options.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Map initiatives into Horizon 1 (core), Horizon 2 (emerging), Horizon 3 (future options).
2. Assess the current resource split and expected value per horizon.
3. Check for gaps: a shrinking H1 with no H2 pipeline.
4. Define different metrics, governance, and funding rules per horizon.
5. Propose a rebalanced portfolio and stage gates.

## Output format
Horizon map, resource split (current vs. target), and metrics/governance per horizon.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Applying H1 metrics (short-term ROI) to H3 ideas; underfunding H2.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
