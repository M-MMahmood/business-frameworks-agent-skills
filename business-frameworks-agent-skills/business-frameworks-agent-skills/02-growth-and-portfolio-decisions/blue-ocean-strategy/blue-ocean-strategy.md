# Blue Ocean Strategy

## Purpose
Use this skill to find uncontested market space and make competition irrelevant.

## When to use
- The user names Blue Ocean Strategy explicitly.
- The task matches: find uncontested market space and make competition irrelevant.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Draw the strategy canvas: key competing factors vs. offering level for you and rivals.
2. Apply the Four Actions: Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create.
3. Look across alternative industries, buyer groups, and complementary offerings.
4. Define the new value curve and test value innovation (differentiation + low cost).
5. Plan validation experiments.

## Output format
Strategy canvas description, ERRC grid, new value proposition, and validation plan.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Only incremental differentiation; ignoring cost implications.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
