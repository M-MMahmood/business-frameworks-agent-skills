# Ansoff Matrix

## Purpose
Use this skill to evaluate growth options by risk.

## When to use
- The user names Ansoff Matrix explicitly.
- The task matches: evaluate growth options by risk.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Define current products and markets.
2. Generate options in four cells: market penetration, market development, product development, diversification.
3. Estimate potential, investment, risk, and time to impact for each.
4. Rank and sequence options; define the first experiments.

## Output format
2x2 with options per cell, a scoring table, and a recommended growth sequence.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Jumping to diversification without exhausting lower-risk cells.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
