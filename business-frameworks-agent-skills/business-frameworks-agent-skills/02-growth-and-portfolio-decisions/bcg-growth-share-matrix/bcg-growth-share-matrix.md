# BCG Growth-Share Matrix

## Purpose
Use this skill to decide how to allocate capital across a portfolio of businesses or products.

## When to use
- The user names BCG Growth-Share Matrix explicitly.
- The task matches: decide how to allocate capital across a portfolio of businesses or products.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. List business units/products with revenue, market growth rate, and relative market share.
2. Classify: Stars (high/high), Cash Cows (low growth/high share), Question Marks (high growth/low share), Dogs (low/low).
3. Assign strategy: invest, harvest, selectively invest or fix, divest.
4. Check cash-flow balance across the portfolio.

## Output format
Classification table, bubble-chart description, and capital allocation recommendation.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Using wrong market definition; ignoring synergies between units.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
