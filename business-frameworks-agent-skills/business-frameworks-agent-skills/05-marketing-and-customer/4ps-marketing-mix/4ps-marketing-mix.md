# 4Ps Marketing Mix

## Purpose
Use this skill to design or diagnose a marketing plan.

## When to use
- The user names 4Ps Marketing Mix explicitly.
- The task matches: design or diagnose a marketing plan.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Product: features, quality, brand, range.
2. Price: strategy, value perception, competitor benchmarks.
3. Place: channels, distribution, coverage.
4. Promotion: messaging, media, budget.
5. Check coherence of the four with the target segment and positioning; identify gaps and recommendations.

## Output format
4P table (current, issues, recommendation) and a coherence assessment.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Treating Ps independently; ignoring the target customer.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
