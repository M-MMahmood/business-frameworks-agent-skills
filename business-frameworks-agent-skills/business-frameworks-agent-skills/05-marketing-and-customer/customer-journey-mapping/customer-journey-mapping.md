# Customer Journey Mapping

## Purpose
Use this skill to find where the customer experience breaks and creates value.

## When to use
- The user names Customer Journey Mapping explicitly.
- The task matches: find where the customer experience breaks and creates value.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Define the persona and the goal of the journey.
2. List stages (awareness, consideration, purchase, onboarding, usage, loyalty).
3. For each: touchpoints, customer actions, thoughts, emotions, pain points.
4. Identify moments of truth and drop-off points.
5. Prioritize fixes by impact and effort; define metrics.

## Output format
Journey table by stage and a prioritized opportunity list.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Mapping the internal process instead of the customer's view; no data.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
