# Net Promoter Score

## Purpose
Use this skill to measure and improve customer loyalty.

## When to use
- The user names Net Promoter Score explicitly.
- The task matches: measure and improve customer loyalty.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Ask: 'How likely are you to recommend us (0-10)?' plus an open-ended 'why'.
2. Classify: Promoters (9-10), Passives (7-8), Detractors (0-6).
3. Compute NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors.
4. Segment by customer type, channel, and journey stage; trend over time.
5. Theme the verbatims, close the loop with detractors, and set improvement actions.

## Output format
NPS value, segment breakdown, top themes, and action list.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Treating the score as the goal; low response rates; no follow-up.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
