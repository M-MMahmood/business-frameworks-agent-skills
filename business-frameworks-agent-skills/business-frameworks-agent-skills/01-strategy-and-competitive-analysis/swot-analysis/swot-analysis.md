# SWOT Analysis

## Purpose
Use this skill to create a structured snapshot of internal capabilities and external conditions.

## When to use
- The user names SWOT Analysis explicitly.
- The task matches: create a structured snapshot of internal capabilities and external conditions.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Clarify the objective or decision the SWOT supports.
2. List Strengths and Weaknesses (internal, controllable) with evidence.
3. List Opportunities and Threats (external, uncontrollable).
4. Prioritize: keep the top 3-5 items per quadrant by impact.
5. Cross-link into a TOWS matrix: SO (use strengths to seize opportunities), WO, ST, WT strategies.

## Output format
2x2 grid plus a TOWS table of concrete strategic options and a recommended priority.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Generic lists with no evidence; mixing internal and external factors; stopping before deriving actions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
