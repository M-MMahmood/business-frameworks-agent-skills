# PESTEL Analysis

## Purpose
Use this skill to scan macro-environment forces affecting a market or company.

## When to use
- The user names PESTEL Analysis explicitly.
- The task matches: scan macro-environment forces affecting a market or company.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Define the market, geography, and time horizon (e.g., 3-5 years).
2. Gather factors for Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Environmental, Legal.
3. Score each factor on impact (1-5) and likelihood/certainty (1-5).
4. Flag the critical few and distinguish trends from uncertainties.
5. State implications for strategy, risks, and monitoring indicators.

## Output format
Six-row table (factor, evidence, impact, likelihood, implication) and a ranked top-5 list of critical forces.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Long unprioritized lists; outdated data; ignoring cross-factor interactions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
