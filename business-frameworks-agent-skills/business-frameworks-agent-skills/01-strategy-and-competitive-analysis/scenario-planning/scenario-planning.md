# Scenario Planning

## Purpose
Use this skill to prepare strategy for uncertain futures.

## When to use
- The user names Scenario Planning explicitly.
- The task matches: prepare strategy for uncertain futures.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Define the decision and time horizon.
2. Identify key driving forces; separate predictable trends from critical uncertainties.
3. Pick the two most impactful, most uncertain drivers and build a 2x2 of four scenarios.
4. Narrate each scenario and its early-warning signals.
5. Stress-test strategy in each: find no-regret moves, hedges, and options to defer.
6. Define triggers and monitoring cadence.

## Output format
Scenario 2x2, short narratives, strategy-robustness matrix, and signposts list.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Building scenarios that are just optimistic/base/pessimistic; no link to decisions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
