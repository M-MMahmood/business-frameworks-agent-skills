# Porter's Generic Strategies

## Purpose
Use this skill to choose between cost leadership, differentiation, and focus.

## When to use
- The user names Porter's Generic Strategies explicitly.
- The task matches: choose between cost leadership, differentiation, and focus.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Define the competitive scope (broad vs. narrow target).
2. Evaluate cost leadership feasibility (scale, process, input advantages).
3. Evaluate differentiation feasibility (willingness to pay, uniqueness sources).
4. Evaluate focus (cost focus or differentiation focus) for a niche.
5. Check for being 'stuck in the middle' and recommend one primary strategy with required capabilities.

## Output format
Option comparison table, chosen strategy, required capabilities, and risks.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Pursuing cost and differentiation without the capabilities for either.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
