# 3Cs Framework

## Purpose
Use this skill to frame a strategy around Customers, Competitors, and the Company.

## When to use
- The user names 3Cs Framework explicitly.
- The task matches: frame a strategy around Customers, Competitors, and the Company.

## Before you start
Confirm or ask for: the company/market/context, the decision being made, the time horizon, and any available data. If data is missing, state assumptions explicitly and label them.

## Process
1. Customers: segments, needs, size, growth, purchase criteria.
2. Competitors: key players, share, positioning, strengths, likely moves.
3. Company: capabilities, costs, assets, brand, constraints.
4. Find the overlap where customer needs are underserved by competitors and fit company strengths.
5. Recommend a positioning and the next validation steps.

## Output format
Three-column fact base, a gap/opportunity statement, and a strategic recommendation.
Lead with the answer or key insight first, then supporting detail. Keep the structure MECE and quantify wherever possible.

## Common pitfalls
Treating the three Cs separately instead of synthesizing; ignoring indirect competitors.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Conclusions are evidence-based and sources or assumptions are stated.
- [ ] Output ends with clear, prioritized recommendations ("so what").
- [ ] Categories do not overlap and nothing material is missing.
